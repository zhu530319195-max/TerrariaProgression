0.14.0 — 全服经验共享 / Server-wide experience sharing
新增服务器开关“全服经验共享”，默认关闭。开启后所有在线且角色数据就绪的玩家各得完整合法击杀经验，不限距离、队伍或是否参战，死亡等待复活也可领取。无离线补发，参战者不重复领，关闭恢复伤害贡献分配。存档v3、协议17和100项天赋保持；本批待实机验收。
Added optional server-wide experience sharing (off by default). Each online initialized player receives the full eligible kill reward once, regardless of team, distance, participation or death. No offline catch-up or extra contributor reward. Off retains damage-based allocation. Save v3, protocol17 and 100 talents retained; live playtesting pending.
