无尽潜能 · Boundless Potential

在冒险中积累经验，自由搭配天赋，让战斗、采集与生产能力不断成长。
击败敌人升级，使用天赋点培养角色。100项天赋覆盖属性、战斗、资源获取、采集与农业；数值天赋默认无限升级，可调当前强度、批量加点，并按实际投入退款。服务器可统一或逐项限制等级。

P：天赋面板；左Alt：开关批量采集；G：切换范围／矿脉；K：开关额外挖矿保护（默认关闭）。

Earn experience from defeated enemies and shape your character with 100 talents across combat, gathering and farming. Numeric talents are uncapped by default, with adjustable strength, bulk purchases and refunds based on points actually paid. Servers can set global and per-talent level limits.

P: talents. Left Alt: toggle batch gathering. G: area/vein mode. K: extra mining protection (off by default).
